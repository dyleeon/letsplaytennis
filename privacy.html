Let's Play Tennis

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Let's Play Tennis ("the app") is a free, browser-based tennis score tracker. This page explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Information We Collect

Let's Play Tennis does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. The app operates entirely within your browser.

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No name, email, or personal identifiers are collected.
  • No location data is accessed.

2. Local Storage

The app uses your browser's localStorage to save your current match score locally on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device.
  • Is automatically cleared when a match ends.
  • Can be deleted at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Cookies

Let's Play Tennis itself does not use cookies. However, third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) may place cookies on your device to serve relevant advertisements.

5. Third-Party Links

The app does not contain links to third-party websites beyond those mentioned in this policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

6. Children's Privacy

Let's Play Tennis is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us via the GitHub repository associated with this project.
